# AGENTS.md

- Radar newsletter subscribe/confirm/unsubscribe/send runs only through the `radar-newsletter` edge function; `radar_assinantes` has no anon/client write access — keeps consent records, rate limits and e-mail sending server-side.
- Public /radar reads comunicados only through the `radar_comunicados_publicos` RPC (active + `publico`), never via an anon table policy — keeps author ids and internal notices private.
- Radar newsletter e-mails send comunicados without images (user choice; hotlinked/copied images rendered broken in mail clients).
- Radar Google News links are swapped for the real article URL in two steps: the DB (pg_net) fetches the Google article page for its signature and the `radar-resolver-links` edge function does the decode POST — Google rate-limits edge/sandbox IPs on the page GET and pg_net cannot send form posts; runs hourly in small batches because bursts get 429.
- Radar /radar visits are recorded only through `radar-newsletter` (`acao: visita`): city/UF from ipwho.is (geojs fallback), device, origin and a random per-browser id; the raw IP is never stored (only a salted SHA-256 for unique counting). Subscribers are identified only when they arrive via e-mail links carrying `r=<token>`.
- Radar campaigns (`acao: campanha`, admin) go only to `status = 'ativo'` subscribers with `aceite_comunicacoes`, always with the unsubscribe link and List-Unsubscribe headers.
- Radar campaigns can also go to prospects (`radar_prospects`, imported in the gestão page; `publico`: assinantes | prospects | todos). Prospects get their own footer and an unsubscribe link with their own token (`mudarStatus` falls back to `radar_prospects` on cancel → `descadastrado`); an e-mail that exists in `radar_assinantes` (any status, so cancelled subscribers too) is never sent as a prospect, and nobody gets the same campaign twice.
- Radar campaigns can include comunicados (`comunicado_ids`, alongside `noticia_ids`, max 10 together): only `ativo` + `publico` ("interno e externo") ones are listed and accepted server-side, sent first and without images, like the daily newsletter.
- Radar campaign metrics: one `radar_campanha_envios` row per recipient + `radar_campanha_eventos`; opens via a 1x1 pixel and clicks via `radar-newsletter?acao=clique` (GET) with an HMAC of envio+URL (no open redirect; the unsubscribe link is never wrapped). Clicks to our own site get `ce=<envio>` so `assinar` attributes the subscription (fallback: same e-mail within 30 days); confirm/cancel mark `assinou_em`/`descadastrou_em`. Delivery/bounce/spam come from the Resend webhook (`POST ?acao=resend`, Svix signature with `RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET`). Tracking is best-effort: a failure never blocks sending or the redirect. Dashboard: `/radar/gestao/campanhas/:id` (RPC `radar_campanha_metricas`).
- Campaign "Inscreva-se" button points to `/radar?assinar=1` (opens the signup form); `/radar/newsletter` is only for confirm/cancel links with a token.
- Radar campaigns are a queue (Resend free plan: 100 e-mails/day for the WHOLE account, reset at 00:00 UTC, shared with Uon1 Sign, confirmations and the daily newsletter): recipients are inserted as `pendente` in `radar_campanha_envios`; `processarCampanha` sends up to the campaign's `limite_diario` and never more than `radar_envio_config.cota_diaria - reserva - sent today (email_historico)`; cron `radar-campanhas-fila` (hourly 9h-18h BRT) calls `acao: continuar_campanhas`; a 429/quota answer puts the batch back in the queue. `cota_diaria = 0` = no daily limit (paid plan). Admin controls: `acao: campanha_controle` (pausar/retomar/limite/enviar_agora/cancelar_fila), `acao: cota` / `envio_config`.
- Radar visits and campaign opens/clicks from robots are not counted: `motivoRobo` (bot user-agent, random utm_source from link scanners, hosting/cloud/e-mail-security provider from ipwho.is `connection`, or outside Brazil for page visits). Visits are kept with `descartada` + `motivo_descarte` (auditable via "Ver descartadas"), and other visits from the same ip_hash within 10 min are discarded too; Gmail/Yahoo image proxies count as real opens.
- Vangard brand images: screens use `src/components/marca/LogoVangard` (files in `public/images/`); e-mails, PDFs and push use `supabase/functions/_shared/marca-vangard.ts` (public `app-config/marca/` storage URLs). On a brand change upload new files under NEW names and update those constants — mail clients cache images by URL and e-mails must not depend on the site being published.
- Radar relevance is decided by `mercado_radar_relevante()` (focus: vehicle protection, not home/life/health): strong words alone pass, weak words ("proteção patrimonial", "cooperativas de seguro"...) need a second weak word or a vehicle/mutual context word, and exclusion words (residencial, imóvel, saúde...) drop the item unless a context word is present. Lists live in `mercado_radar_config` (palavras/fracas/contexto/excluir).
- WhatsApp media (Central WhatsApp) is downloaded server-side from Meta into the PRIVATE `whatsapp-midia` bucket (created on demand by the edge function, no migration); `whatsapp_messages.media_url` stores `storage://whatsapp-midia/<path>` and screens get 1h signed URLs only via the `whatsapp-midia` function, which reads messages with the user's JWT (RLS) — Meta media URLs need the token and expire in minutes, and customer media (documents, photos) must not be public.
